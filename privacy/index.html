Bismuth Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Bismuth Studio is a drawing app for iPad made by Kioura Collective. This policy explains what happens to

your information when you use it. The short version: the app collects nothing automatically. We only receive what you choose to send us by email.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. Bismuth Studio has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your drawings, your settings or any information about you on its own. The only information we receive through the app is what you choose to email us, described under Diagnostics and feedback.

Where your drawings live

  • Your drawings are saved on your iPad.
  • If you choose a folder in iCloud Drive, they are saved there too, in your own iCloud account. Apple

stores them under Apple's privacy policy; we can't see them.

  • Widgets can show your recent drawings and their names. Those pictures stay on your iPad, and Recent Drawings hides them while your iPad is locked.

Photos

When you import a reference image or preview a design on a photo, you pick the photo with Apple's photo

picker. The app only sees the photos you choose. They stay on your iPad unless they appear in a screenshot you choose to send us.

Printing

Print Stencil uses Apple's AirPrint. The printed page goes from your iPad to your printer.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your

payment details. The app checks with the App Store whether the unlock is yours.

Diagnostics and feedback

  • Apple may send us anonymous crash reports if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in

your iPad's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements).

  • The app keeps a short log and, depending on your choice in Preferences › About › Help Grow Bismuth, crash

and performance reports from Apple's MetricKit, plus the app's own speed measurements (timings, memory use, drawing size and the name of the tool in use), on your iPad. Nothing is sent automatically.

  • If you tap Send Feedback, the app opens an email for you to review before sending. It attaches the

build number, your iPad model and iPadOS version, the app's log and the reports you agreed to share, and

a screenshot only if you choose one. The log and reports do not include your drawings or their names. A screenshot shows whatever is on screen, which can include your drawing or a photo. A coming update hides your artwork from feedback screenshots unless you choose to include it.

  • Feedback Mode (test versions only). The screen is captured only when you tap Add Note, so you can mark it up, cover anything private and add a note. Notes stay on your iPad, are left out of its backups, and are deleted after 30 days if you do not send them. Nothing is sent until you review and send the email.
  • If you dictate a note, you are using the dictation built into your iPad's keyboard, which Apple provides. The app receives only the text and does not use the microphone.
  • We never show artwork that you send us to AI, and we never use it to train AI. An email that includes your artwork is not read with Claude.
  • Please ask first before sending us a picture of another person.
  • We use what you send only to fix problems and improve the app. We do not sell it. It is held in Gmail, a service of Google. Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, reads your words and hidden-artwork screenshots only if you switch on Let Claude Read This Feedback when you send them. That switch is off by default. If you switch it on, we may use Claude to read, sort and summarise that email. See Google's privacy policy and Anthropic's privacy policy. We delete feedback emails within 12 months. We may keep short notes about problems we fix in our development records, without your name or email address. To ask us to delete your feedback sooner, email bismuthstudio.feedback@gmail.com.

Lessons and update notices

The app checks GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, about once a day for new lessons, news and notices that a newer version is available. Like any web request, this shares your IP address and basic connection details with that host. It does not send your drawings, your settings or an account.

Children

Bismuth Studio is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact
