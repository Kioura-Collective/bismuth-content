Known Issues

Problems we know about and are working on, with a way around them where there is one.

Masks Can Look Confusing in Print StencilKnown since build 126

Masks can show unexpected marks in the Print Stencil preview. A full review of masks is planned.

Clipping works as expected and can often be used instead.

Live Trace Can Miss Parts of a PictureKnown since build 117

Live Trace, an experimental tool, can leave some areas of a photo untraced.

Adjust the trace settings, or trace the missing parts by hand.

Restore Purchase in Test BuildsKnown since build 128

Purchases cannot be restored in TestFlight builds. Everything is already open for testers.

New Drawing Can Close the App in InchesKnown since build 115

In the New Drawing sheet, tapping the Width or Height box after switching Units to in can close the app.

Pick a preset such as Letter or Tabloid, or set the size in mm or px.
