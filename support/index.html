Bismuth Studio Support

Get help

Email bismuthstudio.feedback@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days. In the app,

Preferences › About › Send Feedback fills in your build and iPad details for you.

Common questions

I bought Bismuth Full but it says Free.

Open Preferences › Bismuth Full and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same

Apple Account you bought it with.

Where are my drawings saved?

On your iPad, and in the iCloud Drive folder you chose, if you chose one (Gallery › Choose Folder).

Can I use my drawings commercially, for example as tattoos?

Yes. What you draw is yours.

Which Apple Pencil do I need?

Any Apple Pencil works, and so does your finger. Apple Pencil Pro adds squeeze for the radial menu and

barrel roll for the ink nib.

What are Experimental tools?

Tools still being shaped: Lattice, Live Trace, 3D Body Preview and Preview on a Photo. Switch them on in

Preferences › Experimental. They may change between updates.

How do I print a stencil at the right size?

Open Print Stencil, set Width on Skin, turn on Mirror for Transfer if your transfer paper needs it, and

print at 100% (no "fit to page" in the print dialog).
